Spiritual Gifts in the Bible

The Bible lists various spiritual gifts in passages like Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12, and Ephesians 4. Here are some of the key gifts given to the Church:

Teaching & Wisdom

The ability to understand deep truths of God's Word and explain them clearly so others can learn and grow in their faith.

Serving & Helps

A passion for doing practical, behind-the-scenes tasks that support others and keep the church running smoothly.

Encouragement (Exhortation)

The gift of motivating, comforting, and lifting up others, especially when they are going through difficult seasons.

Leadership & Administration

The ability to cast a vision, organize people, and guide a group toward a common goal for God's kingdom.

Mercy

Deep compassion and empathy for those who are suffering, combined with a desire to comfort and walk alongside them.

Giving

A joyful, generous spirit that loves to share resources, time, and money to bless others and support ministry.

Evangelism

A natural ability and deep desire to share the good news of Jesus with people who don't know Him yet.

Pastoring (Shepherding)

The heart to care for, protect, and spiritually guide a specific group of believers over a long period of time.
