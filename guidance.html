Dating & Relationships

Culture treats relationships like a trial run for intimacy, but Scripture calls us to guard our hearts (Proverbs 4:23). Focus on becoming the right person before obsessing over finding the right person. "Do not be yoked together with unbelievers" (2 Corinthians 6:14) isn't a restriction to ruin your fun; it's a protection to keep your life's foundation solid.

Lust & Pornography

Lust and pornography are silent destroyers, rewiring how your brain views human beings—turning people made in the image of God into objects for consumption (Matthew 5:28). But hear this: there is zero condemnation for those in Christ Jesus (Romans 8:1). You cannot fight this alone. You need radical accountability (James 5:16).

Anxiety & Worry

Anxiety is a real, heavy burden. God gave us both prayer and therapy. Scripture also gives us a weapon against worry: "Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you" (1 Peter 5:7). Stop trying to control the future, and start trusting the Character of the One who holds it (Philippians 4:6-7).

Is it a sin to Doubt?

Doubt isn't the opposite of faith; it's often a stepping stone to a stronger faith. Even John the Baptist had a moment of doubt in prison (Matthew 11:3). Jude 1:22 says, "Be merciful to those who doubt." Bring your hard questions to the leaders here. The truth has nothing to fear from your questions.

Why does God allow Suffering?

We live in a fractured, fallen world. Jesus said, "In this world you will have trouble" (John 16:33). But we worship a God who stepped into the suffering with us on the cross, and promises to eventually wipe every tear from our eyes (Revelation 21:4). Our present sufferings cannot compare to the glory coming (Romans 8:18).
