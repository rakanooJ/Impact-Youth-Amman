Real Talk & Guidance

Navigating the tough questions and daily challenges together.

School is heavy, but your worth isn't tied to your GPA. Create a firm cut-off time for studying each night. Protect your sleep, and don't be afraid to step away from the desk to go for a walk. Remember, God cares more about your character and your peace than your transcript.

Every single Christian goes through dry seasons—it is completely normal. Don't rely purely on "feelings" to dictate your faith. Stick to the simple habits: keep reading, keep praying (even if it feels like you're talking to the ceiling), and keep showing up to community. The feelings usually catch up to the habits.

You become like the five people you spend the most time with. If a friendship constantly makes you compromise your values or leaves you feeling drained, it's okay to create boundaries. You don't have to be mean, but you do have to protect your peace. Lean into the community here at Impact.

Absolutely. Doubt isn't the opposite of faith; it's often an invitation to deeper understanding. Bring your hard questions to the leaders here. We aren't scared of your questions, and neither is God. It's much better to wrestle with the hard truths than to pretend you have it all figured out.
