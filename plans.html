Mini Study (Mark) - 7 Days to Think Deeper

Day 1
Mark 1-2
Questions to Think About:
  • Mark launches straight into Jesus’ public ministry at the start of his gospel. Why do you think he writes this way?
  • Which healing stands out to you the most and why?
  • What was revolutionary about what Jesus said and did in Mark 2:18-27?
Day 2
Mark 3-4
Questions to Think About:
  • What does the person say about Jesus’s authority (the way he can give a command and be obeyed straight away)?
  • Why did Jesus tell parables? What do they mean?
Day 3
Mark 5-7
Questions to Think About:
  • What do you think the people Jesus interacted with thought of him?
  • What do you think Jesus means in Mark 7:15?
Day 4
Mark 8-9
Questions to Think About:
  • What did Jesus say about the importance of his death, Mark 8:31-38?
  • What was the significance of Jesus’s transfiguration in Mark 9:2-13?
Day 5
Mark 10-12
Questions to Think About:
  • Which of the events in these chapters would have been most impactful to witness?
  • What does the great commandment in Mark 12:29–30 mean for you today?
Day 6
Mark 13-14
Questions to Think About:
  • What do you make of chapter 13 and the prophecies that Jesus speaks?
  • What comments can you make about Jesus’s attitude in the run up to his death?
Day 7
Mark 15-16
Questions to Think About:
  • Which details of the crucifixion do you think are most significant?
  • What would it have been like to be present at each of Jesus‘s appearances after his resurrection?

Quick Guide

Got 5 minutes?

If you’ve only got a few minutes, that’s cool. It’s worth picking a story from the gospel or a Psalm to focus on – something punchy and short. The key is to make space before and after to process what you’ve read and be creative.

Got 15 minutes?

15 minutes is a good amount of time to go through a chapter of the Bible. It’s probably best not to grab a chapter at random, though. Start at the beginning of a new book (like Mark). Pray first, read a chapter, stop for a minute, then read it again. On the second time, underline, ask questions, color, and imagine!

Got 30 minutes?

You can read a long chapter or a few chapters back to back. Or you can go really deep on one chapter, or even one verse. Try memorizing a few verses, or close your eyes and keep repeating a passage, pulling out different words. Meditating like this could change your life!
