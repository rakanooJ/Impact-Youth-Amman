The 5 Core Romans 12 Gifts Tested

This quiz analyzes your responses based on five of the primary grace gifts outlined by Paul in Romans 12:6-8.

🗣️ Exhortation (Encouragement)

The ability to come alongside others to offer comfort, motivation, and uplifting counsel to strengthen their faith.

📖 Teaching

The ability to understand, clearly explain, and apply the truths of God's Word so that others can grasp deep theological concepts.

🛠️ Serving (Helps)

A deep joy in recognizing practical needs and stepping in to do the behind-the-scenes work to support the church body.

🚀 Leadership (Administration)

The God-given ability to cast a vision, organize resources, and gently guide a group toward a specific, kingdom-focused goal.

💛 Mercy (Compassion)

An exceptional empathy and genuine compassion for those who are suffering physically, emotionally, or spiritually.
